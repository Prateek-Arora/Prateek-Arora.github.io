To get new posts as they come out, add this address to a feed reader:

`https://prateek-arora.github.io/feed.xml`

Any reader works, for example NetNewsWire, Feedly or Inoreader. You can also paste the address of
this page or the home page, and the reader will find the feed.

I also share new posts on [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/aroraprateek.bsky.social) and
[Mastodon](https://mastodon.social/@prateekarora).
